Frequently asked questions

How do I create or join a dining invitation?

Open the app, tap the create button to post a dining invitation, or browse open invitations near you and tap to join. You'll be notified when someone responds.

How is my privacy protected?

Privacy is built in. We show an age category — never your exact age — and we never display your phone number, email, or precise location. You choose what to share on your profile.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account inside the app. This permanently removes your account and associated data. If you can't access the app, email us at support@dinebuddies.com and we'll assist.

How do I report a user or content?

Use the report option (the ⋯ menu) on a profile, post, or card. Our team reviews reports and takes action on anything that breaks our guidelines.

I have a business — how do I get listed?

Create a business account in the app, then complete your profile (name, type, and Google location). Businesses get a profile, special offers, job postings, a live Stage, analytics, and a direct feedback inbox.

How do Dine Credits work?

Dine Credits are used for optional in-app features. You can top up your balance anytime from your wallet in the app. Credits are separate from any subscription.
