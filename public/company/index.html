What we build

DineBuddies is a social dining platform that connects people, meals, and the local venues worth gathering around.

🍽️

Social dining invitations

Host or join a meal — find good company for lunch, dinner, or coffee near you.

🧭

Discovery & matching

Meet compatible dining buddies nearby through privacy-first profiles and smart discovery.

🏪

Venue partnerships

Restaurants, cafés, bars and hotels get a profile, offers, and a direct line to diners.

💬

Community & live rooms

Community chat and live Stage rooms bring people together around shared tables.

🎮

Social games

Playful compatibility games help break the ice before you ever sit down.

🔒

Safety & privacy

Photo verification and a privacy-first design keep the community trusted and respectful.
